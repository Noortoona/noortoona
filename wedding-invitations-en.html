Four checks before you share your wedding invitation

  1. Check the spelling of both names and the event date.
  2. Add the venue name and an accurate map link.
  3. Review text size and readability on a mobile phone.
  4. Test the guest link and confirm which sending and entry services are available.

Automatic sending and notifications depend on service activation. Confirm availability with the team before relying on them for your event.
